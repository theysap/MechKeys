Sound recordings bundled with MechKeys
======================================

The keypress recordings in MechKeys.app/Contents/Resources/Sounds are
recordings of real mechanical keyboards by Thomas Lai (tplai), published as
part of kbsim and distributed through the thock-soundpacks registry under the
MIT licence. MechKeys resamples them to 48 kHz, trims leading digital silence,
applies short boundary fades and normalises their level; the recordings
themselves are unchanged.

Switch packs used:

    Gateron Ink Red          -> the "Red" profile
    Drop Holy Panda          -> the "Brown" profile
    Kailh Box Navy           -> the "Blue" profile
    Gateron Ink Black        -> the "Black" profile
    NovelKeys Cream          -> the "Yellow" profile

Switch names identify the recording. No manufacturer endorsement is implied,
and MechKeys is not affiliated with any of them.
